# Product

## Register

product

## Users
PathGuru operators, editorial leads, and content assistants managing blog workflows inside an internal command center. They work in long sessions across generation, manual writing, review, and scheduling, often under time pressure.

## Product Purpose
PathGuru coordinates end-to-end publishing work in one operational interface. Success means users can move from draft intent to scheduled or published output quickly, with clear status visibility and minimal interaction friction.

## Brand Personality
Confident, focused, and practical. The interface should feel like a reliable operations tool: calm under load, clear in structure, and direct in feedback.

## Anti-references
- Over-decorated dashboard UIs with heavy glow/gradient styling.
- Dense control bars that hide hierarchy and force trial-and-error.
- Interfaces that trap content in fixed panels without obvious scrolling behavior.

## Design Principles
- Prioritize workflow clarity over visual novelty.
- Keep primary actions visible, and secondary controls progressive.
- Reduce cognitive load with clear grouping and consistent spacing.
- Preserve context while editing with sticky utility controls.
- Make vertical progress obvious through reliable, predictable scrolling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard operation, and focus visibility. Avoid motion-heavy transitions, preserve readable control sizes, and ensure content remains reachable at common desktop and laptop breakpoints.
