# DigiFusion Brand Guidelines

## Visual Identity & Color Palette
- **Primary / Accent Color:** `#00D6AB` (Electric Teal / Mint)
- **Secondary / Link Color:** `#7C3AED` (Deep Violet)
- **Background:** `#0A0A0A` (Dark Charcoal / Near Black)
- **Text Primary:** `#F2F2F2` (Off-White)
- **Color Scheme:** Modern, high-contrast dark mode

## Typography
- **Headings:** `Instrument Serif` (Serif, elegant, authoritative)
- **Body & Paragraphs:** `DM Sans` (Clean, modern sans-serif)
- **Monospace / Technical:** `JetBrains Mono`

## Voice, Tone & Persona
- **Tone:** Authoritative, direct, technical yet accessible, modern
- **Energy:** High, execution-focused
- **Key Phrases:** "Predictable revenue, by design", "Diagnosis before prescription", "Authority that converts, not content that fills space"
- **Style:** Clear value delivery, ROI-driven, avoiding generic marketing fluff
