## CATEGORY 1: How to Win Government Contracts in Nigeria

**Topic 1 — How NOCOPO Actually Works (And Why Most Contractors Are Using It Wrong)**
- Target keyword: NOCOPO Nigeria guide
- Angle: Most contractors treat NOCOPO as a search bar. This article explains the OCDS data structure, how to read procurement notices properly, which fields matter for bid decisions, and the 5 types of records NOCOPO publishes that most users never look at.
- Research prompt: "Research the Nigeria Open Contracting Portal (NOCOPO) data structure, OCDS standard, what fields are published, how to filter by sector and value, common issues with data completeness, and practical tips for government contractors using it to find opportunities."

**Topic 2 — The 7 Types of Nigerian Government Contracts (And Which Ones You Should Actually Be Chasing)**
- Target keyword: Nigerian government contract types
- Angle: Most contractors bid on works and goods because those are the most visible. This article breaks down all 7 procurement types (works, goods, services, consultancy, ICT, non-consultancy, PPP), which MDAs award each, typical contract durations, and which segments have the least competition.
- Research prompt: "Research the 7 types of government procurement under Nigeria's Public Procurement Act 2007, how each is tendered (ICB, NCB, restricted, sole source), typical value thresholds for each procurement method, and which sectors in Nigeria have the highest frequency of government contracts."

**Topic 3 — How to Read a Nigerian Government Tender Notice: A Line-by-Line Guide**
- Target keyword: how to read government tender Nigeria
- Angle: Step-by-step walkthrough of a real NOCOPO tender notice. Explains every field — reference number, procurement method, eligibility criteria, evaluation criteria, submission requirements — with plain-English commentary on what each means for your bid decision.
- Research prompt: "Find and analyse the structure of a typical Nigerian federal government tender notice from NOCOPO or BPP. What are the standard fields, what do the procurement method codes mean (ICB vs NCB vs RFQ), what eligibility conditions typically appear, and what are the most common reasons bids are disqualified on technical compliance?"

**Topic 4 — Why 70% of Nigerian Government Contractors Lose on Compliance, Not Capability**
- Target keyword: government contract bid disqualification Nigeria
- Angle: Research-backed article on the most common reasons bids are rejected in Nigerian government procurement — wrong document format, missing certifications, wrong financial year accounts, non-compliant company registration — and a compliance checklist BD teams can use before every submission.
- Research prompt: "Research the most common bid disqualification reasons in Nigerian public procurement. What compliance documents are typically required (CAC, FIRS, PENCOM, ITF, NSITF, BPP registration)? What are the rules on financial statements, insurance requirements, and company registration status? Find examples of procurement audits or BPP reports that cite compliance failures."

**Topic 5 — The Real Difference Between Pre-Qualification and Tendering in Nigeria (Most Contractors Confuse Them)**
- Target keyword: pre-qualification vs tendering Nigeria
- Angle: Explains the two-stage procurement process, why pre-qualification shortlisting matters more than most contractors realise, how to build a pre-qualification profile that wins shortlist spots, and which major MDAs use pre-qualification for all major contracts.
- Research prompt: "Explain the pre-qualification process in Nigerian government procurement under the Public Procurement Act. Which MDAs commonly use pre-qualification before open tendering? What criteria are typically used for shortlisting? How does Expression of Interest (EOI) differ from pre-qualification, and how should contractors structure their EOI submissions?"
